# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25.10.2026

Services provided by **Wave Player** are intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The Personal Information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

The terms used in this Privacy Policy have the same meanings as in our Terms and Conditions, which is accessible in the app unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy.

**DATA COLLECTION**\
Wave Player does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.

All audio processing is performed entirely on your local device. Your library, playlists, settings, analysis results, and play history are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

**UPDATE CHECKS**\
On platforms with in-app updates, Wave Player queries the public GitHub Releases API for the latest release (and its published SHA-256 checksums) when it starts and then at most once every 24 hours. These requests contain no personal data, account information, or device identifiers. As with any HTTP request, GitHub receives the IP address the request comes from and the updater's user agent (`wave-player-updater`); GitHub processes this data under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement. Update archives are downloaded only after you confirm an update and are verified against a published SHA-256 checksum before installation.

**THIRD-PARTY SERVICES**\
Wave Player does not use third-party services for analytics, advertising, or data collection. The only external service contacted automatically is GitHub, for update checks and update downloads as described above.

**ADVERTISING NETWORKS**\
Wave Player does not use monetization and has no plans for it in the future.

**CHILDREN’S PRIVACY**\
Wave Player does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, whether child or adult. This means no personally identifiable information is collected from children under 13.
